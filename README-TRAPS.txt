SECOND ME - THE EMBERFORGE

CHAY GAME
1. Giai nen toan bo thu muc.
2. Bam START_GAME.bat. Khong can Node.js.
3. Muon may khac cung mang choi: dung dia chi "May khac" trong cua so server.
4. Neu cong 3000 da duoc dung, tat server game cu truoc khi mo ban nay.

DIEU KHIEN
WASD / mui ten: di chuyen.
R: bat/dung ghi. E: goi Echo. Q: xoa Echo.
J / Space: chem kiem khi danh boss.
Shift: luot ne (hoi 1.2 giay). Esc: tam dung. T: choi lai man.
Echo giu an giua dau truong lam cham boss va tang sat thuong 15 -> 24.
Ra ngoai vong cam truoc khi boss ra don.

LUU TIEN DO
Di vat va kiem duoc luu trong trinh duyet tren may dang choi.
Choi lai man khong nhan trung di vat. Hanh trinh moi se dat lai tien do.
Cac may choi co tien do rieng; day la game don, khong dong bo nguoi choi.

Da qua 40 tests, kiem tra TypeScript, build production va thu lo ren/boss tren giao dien.
Ban nay khong tu cap nhat may 192.168.2.16. Hay chep thu muc moi sang may do.
